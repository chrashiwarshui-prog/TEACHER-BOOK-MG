Privacy Policy for Teacher Book MG
​Last updated: November 27, 2026
​Teacher Book MG is an educational and classroom management application developed by Edelin Manni-Tan for teachers and educators.
​1. Information We Collect
​Teacher Book MG does not require users to create an account or submit sensitive personal information to use its core features.
​Any classroom notes, schedules, lesson plans, or records entered by the user are stored locally on the user's device unless the user explicitly exports or shares them.
​2. Permissions and Local Storage
​The application may request device permissions (such as storage access) only when necessary to save, export, import, or back up files and documents chosen by the user.
​3. Third-Party Services and Links
​If the application or its distribution page includes external links or third-party store services (such as Aptoide), those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.
​4. Children's Privacy
​Teacher Book MG is designed primarily for teachers and educators and does not knowingly collect personal data directly from children.
​5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
​This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as new versions of Teacher Book MG are released. Any updates will be published on the official page.
